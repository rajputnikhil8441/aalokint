About us

A quiet green space for families in Cumilla

APON BARI Eco Park is a locally owned eco park and picnic destination in Keronkhal, Chandina, Cumilla. The park was set up to give families, schools and groups from Cumilla and the surrounding districts an open, green place to spend a relaxed day away from the town.

The grounds are kept simple and natural — grass, trees, shaded seating and room for children to run. Groups can book space for picnics, school excursions, family get-togethers and small outdoor programmes.
